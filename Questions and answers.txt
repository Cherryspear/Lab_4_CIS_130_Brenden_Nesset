1. What are the four main parts of the CSS box model?

	The content box, the padding box, the border box, and the mrgin box.

2. What is the difference between padding and margin?

	Padding creates space inside an element's border, while margin creates space outside of it

3. What does box-sizing: border-box change about how width is calculated?

	It changes the width calculation so that an element's specified width includes its padding and border, rather than adding them on top
